About

I'm a Computer Science student at KL University, Hyderabad, currently in the first year of a B.Tech (2025–2029). Most of what I build starts as a question about how something works underneath — a shell, a data structure, a banking ledger — and turns into a small C or Java program that answers it.

Alongside systems-level work, I build for the browser: React components, plain JavaScript apps and Python scripts for analysing data. I like projects that are small enough to finish and specific enough to teach me something concrete.
